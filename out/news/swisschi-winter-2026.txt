9:T628,<p>On February 10, 2026, members of the ETH Zurich HCI community gathered at the University of Lausanne for the SwissCHI Winter Meeting, the annual gathering of Switzerland's human-computer interaction research community. ETH HCI researchers presented several papers accepted at top venues including CHI 2026 and IUI 2026.</p>
<figure style="max-width: 400px; margin: 0 auto;">
  <img src="/news/swisschi.JPG" alt="Yang Wu presenting at SwissCHI" />
  <figcaption>First-year doctoral student Yang Wu from the PEACH Lab presents her recent IUI paper, "StepMIND."</figcaption>
</figure>
<h2>Program Highlights</h2>
<p>The day-long event featured paper presentations from researchers across Swiss universities, a panel discussion on HCI funding in Switzerland, and breakout sessions on emerging topics including persuasion in the age of LLMs, just-in-time adaptive interventions, digital wellbeing beyond screen time, misinformation resilience, and privacy in personalized systems.</p>
<p>The meeting concluded with a guided tour of the Pyxis museum exhibition "If the snow never returned: a glimpse into a world in flux"</p>
<h2>About SwissCHI</h2>
<p><a href="https://swisschi.acm.org/">SwissCHI</a> brings together HCI researchers from across Switzerland to share their work, discuss funding opportunities, and strengthen connections within the Swiss HCI community. The Winter Meeting was hosted in the newly redesigned SCALE learning space at UNIL's Internef building.</p>
