Prof. Mitterberger joins the Department of Architecture, bringing expertise at the intersection of architecture, human-robot interaction, and computational design. Her research explores how emerging technologies—including robotics and extended reality can transform the way we design and construct the built environment.

We look forward to the collaborations and interdisciplinary connections her work will foster within the broader HCI research community at ETH.

Read more about Prof. Mitterberger's appointment on the [ETH Architecture website](https://arch.ethz.ch/en/news-und-veranstaltungen/news-kanal/2025/12/daniela-mitterberger.html).
